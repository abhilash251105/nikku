chapter one

It was college

Some days were long. The lectures blurred together. The same chai from the canteen, the same walk back, the same playlist on repeat. And then you were there — and suddenly everything had a soundtrack.

I didn't plan it. I didn't see it coming. But one day I caught myself looking for you in every crowd, and I knew I was already in it.

chapter two

1,095 days of us

the first time
I remember the exact moment. Not dramatic. Not movie-scene. Just you, laughing at something stupid I said, and I thought — I want this forever.
the laughter
The late-night calls that turned into morning. The terrible movies we sat through because you picked them. The way you laugh at my jokes even when they're not funny. Especially when they're not funny.
the hard days
We've had them. The kind that test you. And every single time, I'd rather be in the middle of it with you than anywhere else without you.

The way you make any ordinary day feel like it's worth remembering
